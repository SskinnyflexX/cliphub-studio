Last updated: October 9, 2026

ClipHub Studio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use ClipHub Studio and its integrations.

ClipHub Studio may process account authorization information, access tokens, video metadata and technical information required to operate integrations that you explicitly connect. The application is designed to use only the permissions necessary for its configured features.

2. Video content

Video files processed by ClipHub Studio are used to perform the editing, captioning, formatting and publishing functions requested by the user. Users are responsible for the rights and permissions associated with their source material.

3. Third-party platforms

When you connect a third-party platform, that platform may independently process information according to its own privacy policy and terms. ClipHub Studio does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

4. Data retention

ClipHub Studio is designed to retain only information reasonably required for its operation, such as authorization credentials and application state. Users should revoke an integration through the relevant platform when they no longer want the application to have access.

5. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and application data. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may stop using ClipHub Studio and revoke connected platform permissions at any time through the relevant platform. You may also contact the ClipHub Studio team regarding privacy questions.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its integrations change. The latest version will be published on this page.
