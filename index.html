What is ClipHub Studio?

ClipHub Studio is a desktop application designed to simplify the workflow from an authorized source video to a finished short-form video.

The application can help creators select moments, edit clips, adapt videos to vertical formats, generate captions and titles, and prepare content for publication on supported social platforms.

Designed for creators

ClipHub Studio focuses on entertainment, gaming and creator content. It is intended to help creators organize and streamline repetitive editing and publishing tasks while keeping control over their content.

Authorized content

Users are responsible for ensuring that the videos, recordings, clips, music and other material they use with ClipHub Studio are owned by them or otherwise authorized for the intended use.
